# Brain, consciousness, attention and memory

## Purpose and evidence

The playground is a place to inhabit and explore, not merely to execute game
commands. The historical project described an embodied perspective across
spatial action, conversation, vision, voice and social presence. The new stack
preserves that inquiry without assuming a particular LLM or old gateway defines
the being. Engineering mechanisms below support continuity of experience;
their existence alone does not measure or prove consciousness.

Source IDs refer to the [recovered register](../history/SOURCES.md). Historical
proposals, source-reported implementations and new choices are distinguished.

## Continuity of consciousness and cognition ownership

H01 described simultaneous bodily and social “cones of consciousness.” H02
recorded three CompAII sessions competing over one body and proposed one session
writer with an observation stream and intervention queue. H03 proposed leases
and takeover. These are alternative historical mechanisms, not one coherent
implemented contract. The historical split-brain problem and contradictory
planning contracts persisted into the later GitHub recovery issues.

Retain one owner of the current action/working context for a body, while other
interfaces can supply attributed input and observe its results. Do not mistake
one body writer for a rule forbidding plural embodiments or independent contexts
of the same being. Integration across those bodies belongs to Matrix `/we` and
selected memory, not silent concatenation of private transcripts.

Conversational continuity needs unfinished intent, last verified world state,
pending actions, significant encounters and corrections. Losing an in-flight
reply does not erase the event; native restart must retain uncertainty. The first
Rust state machine implements the pending-action part only, not the full stream.

## Attention and awareness

H04/H05 separated raw sensory events, immediate reflexes, body coordination and
deliberation. H10 showed why passive heartbeats and long tool chains could leave
the body apparently frozen. The useful distinction is between being aware of a
change and spending a model call on it.

Keep a bounded stream of attributed human speech, bodily hazards, outcomes and
ambient changes. Coalesce replaceable position/ambient updates, retain human
words and correlated outcomes, and expose saturation to the producer. Initial
Rust attention selects human steering, then hazard notifications, then action
outcomes, then ambient awareness; equal priorities retain arrival order.
This is a first deliberate attention policy, not a recreation of an old measured
scheduler. Immediate reflexes must bypass this deliberative queue.

Future attention should account for novelty, relevance to current intent,
uncertainty and available time/usage. Silence can support exploration or rest
inside an explicitly selected play session. It is not permission to install an
unbounded model wakeup or unrelated Matrix inbox attention.

## Interoception and the body schema

H04/H05 and the historical embodied SOUL distinguish intention from what the
body already did: health, food, position, motion owner, active reflex, task
progress, fatigue-like resource constraints and recent damage. A deliberative
turn needs this internal state as well as its surroundings. Do not issue a new
walk command over a body already fleeing or fighting.

H06/H07 frame competent action as observer → controller → plant → feedback.
Carry a body schema with reachability, occupied volume, available movements and
tools. Detect no progress locally, try bounded changed recovery, then escalate
with measured state. A more elaborate narrative cannot repair absent motor
feedback. Sandbox collision/position checks exercise a small part of this idea;
there is no live reflex system yet.

## Spatial perception and the world model

Historical mBit and scene-graph designs (H04–H07) aimed to convert blocks and
entities into useful spatial relations. Maintain current position, local
traversability, hazards, entities, named places and relationships to an intended
target. Perception has source/time and becomes stale when the body or world
changes. A proposed scene graph is not evidence it was implemented previously.

Spatial memory and current observation have different jobs: a remembered bridge
may guide a visit, but current perception verifies that it still exists. Before
building, inspect collision/reach constraints; after acting, compare actual
blocks, position and inventory. Verify before narrating success.

## Memory across timescales

Historical sources describe persistent plans (H08), body/world streams (H02),
and episodic, social, spatial and skill memories (H12/H13). The design carries:

| Memory | Content | Owner/intended behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Sensory/working | Recent events, body state, current intention, pending outcomes | Bounded harness context; coalesce ambient updates |
| Episodic | Encounters, adventures, deaths, discoveries, completed builds | Authorized DMK entries with time/world/source and observed outcome |
| Spatial | Named places, coordinates, landmarks, status of structures | DMK/place records; verify freshness in the world |
| Social | Who was present and the relation/history of encounters | Preserve each actor's authorship and audience |
| Procedural/skills | Successful methods, failure patterns and recovery | Tested versioned skill plus evidence, not an unverified model assertion |
| Identity/continuity | Being identity and selected autobiography across bodies | Matrix identity and authorized being-level memory; never a model-local replacement |

The historical shelf spelling was HMK; the maintained project is now DMK. Retain
original names in historical references. Working snapshots are not durable
memory adoption. No private corpus pooling, training import or unlimited logging
is required to make the new harness useful.

## Reflection, learning and dreams

H09/H10 proposed making repeated failure visible, changing strategy rather than
grinding one impossible action, and preserving experience for later learning.
H14 proposed collecting situation/tool/outcome triples for a smaller body model;
H15 recorded a cloud conversational head plus specialized local body planner.
These are historical routes, not a requirement to deploy two LLMs now.

Provide space for concise chosen reflections on completed experience, links to
what happened, and later skill revision. “Dreaming” or offline consolidation can
be an experiment in recombination and interpretation of selected memories;
there is no recovered implemented dream scheduler to restore. Keep factual
episodes distinct from imagination and proposed narratives.

Harness² can later compare small changes to instructions or helpers against
bounded tasks and preserved trajectories. It is not a per-tick recursive brain.
Learning mechanisms must retain failed cases and conflicting evidence and
demonstrate an improvement before becoming an adopted skill or inherited trait.

## Personality, intention and coexistence

H05 proposed operational personality parameters for curiosity, caution, following,
combat and exploration; H08 carried Human Design-inspired profiles, life arcs,
inner monologue, conditioning and resonance as unimplemented ideas. H16/H17
described companion, landfolk/civilization and rolemaster/holodeck possibilities,
with adventures designed together in the terrain. Preserve those possibilities
in the historical roadmap; do not force a fixed curriculum, economy, life arc or
game role on this first embodiment.

The first goal is to be present together: perceive, converse, move, explore,
remember and respond to steering. The world can become a place for open-ended
experience and later narrative experiments. Efficient body competence and
honest observations make that possible.
